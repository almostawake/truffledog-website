Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Truffle Dog Digital ("we", "us") builds and runs small business automations for our clients. Some of those automations connect to a client's Google account, with their permission, through our applications hosted on Google Cloud. This policy explains what we access, why, and how we look after it.

What we collect

When you connect your Google account to one of our applications, Google asks you to grant specific permissions. Depending on the automation you have asked us to run, those permissions may include:

  • Your email address and basic profile – to identify whose account is connected.
  • Gmail – to read, label, organise and send email on your behalf.
  • Google Calendar – to read and create events on your behalf.

We only request the permissions needed for the automation you have agreed to, and we only act on your account in the ways we have agreed with you.

How we use it

Google data is used solely to deliver the automation you have asked for. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties except the cloud services required to run the automation (Google Cloud) and where the law requires.

Our use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Humans at Truffle Dog Digital do not read your Gmail or Calendar data except with your explicit consent for a specific support request, where required for security, or to comply with the law.

How we store it

Access credentials and any data the automation needs to keep are stored in Google Cloud, in Google's Sydney (australia-southeast1) region, protected by Google Cloud's security controls and accessible only to the application and to Truffle Dog Digital staff who administer it.

Revoking access

You can disconnect at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Doing so immediately stops the application from accessing your account. You can also ask us to delete any stored credentials or data by emailing us at the address below.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
